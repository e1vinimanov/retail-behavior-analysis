Business interpretation

What to investigate next

  • Review the first 24 hours after cart addition to understand purchase friction and customer intent.
  • Investigate November’s higher volume alongside its lower conversion rate. Product mix, promotions and customer mix are possible explanations, not established causes.
  • If reminder campaigns are available, test their incremental impact with a randomized holdout. These results alone do not establish whether reminders work.
Measurement boundaries

What this does not establish

  • Customer-product pairs are not orders or whole carts. The complement of 19.65% is not a whole-cart abandonment rate.
  • First observed purchase does not mean a customer’s first-ever purchase. No purchase in 30 days does not establish churn.
  • No campaign spend or monetary revenue is used. This analysis cannot establish ROAS, profit or causal marketing impact.

Definitions, source and reproducibility

Source: Synerise RecSys 2025 dataset, with events recorded in 2022. Dashboard values are embedded from the project’s SQL aggregate results. This is an offline HTML report, not a live database connection or a Power BI report.

Read the measurement rules
  • Purchase table: 2,318,502 rows. Cart table: 7,541,117 rows. Exact duplicate event rows were retained because no event or order identifier was available.
  • December 7 is treated as the final complete observation day because December 8 contains only partial-day records. This is an analysis assumption, not proof of uninterrupted tracking.
  • Repeat-purchase eligibility: first observed purchase date on or before November 7, 2022. Same-day purchases do not count as returns.
  • Cart eligibility: earliest observed cart timestamp for each customer-product pair strictly before December 1, 2022. A matching purchase must be strictly later than that timestamp and at most seven days later.
  • Overall rates divide total converted counts by total eligible counts. Monthly percentages are not averaged.
  • Dashboard presentation prepared with AI assistance. SQL and Python analysis should be explained and reproduced from the project repository.
